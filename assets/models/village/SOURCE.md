# Village (CC0)

Built by `tools/spike/export_village.py` from the look-dev builders in `tools/art/wf_build.py`
(turf-roof timber houses, boathouses, clinker boats), placed at the `terrain.json` village spots.
Ambient occlusion baked in Blender Cycles (`village_ao.png`).

Textures in `tex/` (copied by `tools/spike/village_textures.py`, 1024 px) and the pier model are from
Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), licence CC0:
weathered_plank_siding, wood_trunk_wall, leafy_grass, stone_wall, weathered_brown_planks,
wood_planks_dirt, modular_wooden_pier.
