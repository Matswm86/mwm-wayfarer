# Characters (CC0)

Made with MPFB 2 and the MakeHuman system assets (CC0); presets from `tools/art/wf_mh.py`,
exported by `tools/spike/export_human.py`:
- `ingrid.glb`: Ingrid, the dock villager (idle loop).
- `explorer.glb`: the player (idle and walk loops).
Clothing colours re-dyed in the exported textures.
