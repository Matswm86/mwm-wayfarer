# Scots pine (near-camera meshes)

`scots_pine_a.glb`: Poly Haven `pine_tree_01` (CC0, polyhaven.com), turned into a phone tree by the
graphic designer's `tools/assets/cardify_tree.py` (decimated baked trunk + alpha-cut twig cards,
LOD0 about 18k triangles, LOD1 about 2.6k; COLOR_0 = crown occlusion). Copied from
`assets/incoming/scots_pine/` on 2026-10-10. Drawn by `scripts/NearTrees.gd` within 70 m on Ultra.
