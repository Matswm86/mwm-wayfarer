# Tree impostors (CC0)

`trees_albedo.png` / `trees_normal.png`: impostor atlases rendered in Blender Cycles by
`tools/spike/bake_tree_impostors.py` (packed by `tools/spike/pack_tree_atlas.py`) from Poly Haven
models (polyhaven.com, licence CC0): pine_tree_01 (variants a and c), fir_tree_01 (a and b),
island_tree_01 (broadleaf stand-in for birch; Poly Haven has no birch).
